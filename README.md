# Awesome VC Tech Stack [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated collection of operational tools, software, and platforms for Venture Capitalists, Emerging Managers, and Family Offices.

This list is designed to help General Partners (GPs) build a modern efficient stack for sourcing deals, managing portfolio companies, and reporting to Limited Partners.

## Deal Sourcing Engines
*Automated tools to identify breakout startups before the competition.*

* **[VCBacked.co](https://vcbacked.co)** - Signal-based sourcing platform tracking high-velocity venture-backed companies.
* [SourceScrub](https://www.sourcescrub.com) - Deal origination platform for private equity and M&A.
* [Harmonic](https://harmonic.ai) - Database of startups and tech talent for investors.
* [Specter](https://specter.io) - AI-driven talent and company identification for early-stage investors.

## Fundraising & LP Relations
*Tools for GPs to raise capital and manage Limited Partner relationships.*

* **[LPBacked.com](https://lpbacked.com)** - Intelligence for identifying and connecting with active Limited Partners (LPs) and Family Offices.
* [DocSend](https://www.docsend.com) - Secure document sharing for pitch decks and LP updates.
* [Mercury](https://mercury.com) - Banking built for startups and venture funds.
* [Passthrough](https://www.passthrough.com) - Automated subscription documents and fund closing software.

## Co-Investment & Syndicates
*Resources for finding co-investors, angels, and syndicate partners.*

* **[AngelBacked.co](https://angelbacked.co)** - Track angel activity to find co-investment opportunities and emerging scout networks.
* [Sydecar](https://www.sydecar.io) - Frictionless deal execution platform for syndicates and SPVs.
* [Vauban](https://www.vauban.io) - Platform to launch and manage SPVs and Venture Funds online.
* [AngelList Stack](https://www.angellist.com/stack) - Suite of tools for managing investments and roll-up vehicles.

## Fund Administration & Cap Tables
* [Carta](https://carta.com) - Equity management, 409A valuations, and fund administration.
* [Pulley](https://pulley.com) - Cap table management designed for high-growth startups.
* [Aumni](https://www.aumni.fund) - Investment analytics platform for private capital data.

## CRM & Relationship Management
* [Affinity](https://affinity.co) - Relationship intelligence CRM used heavily by VCs to manage deal flow.
* [Attio](https://attio.com) - Fully customizable CRM for modern startups and funds.
* [Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com) - Sales CRM often adapted for deal flow tracking.

## Contributing

We welcome contributions from the community! If you use a tool that helps run your fund, please submit a Pull Request.

2.  Create your feature branch (`git checkout -b feature/NewTool`)
3.  Commit your changes (`git commit -m 'Add NewTool'`)
4.  Push to the branch (`git push origin feature/NewTool`)
5.  Open a Pull Request
